Honeyed Berry Parfait

A sweet little start to your day

About This Dish

Creamy yoghurt layered with juicy blueberries and a delicate drizzle of golden honey. This simple, refreshing parfait is a lovely way to begin your morning.

Ingredients

  • Yoghurt
  • Honey
  • Blueberries

Tip: Layer the yoghurt and blueberries in a glass, then drizzle honey over the top. Adjust the quantities to suit your taste.
